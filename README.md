# NIST CSF 2.0 Gap Assessment

A simulated security gap assessment for Garden State Sports, a fictional sports training business with 20 employees.

## Assessment
Reviewed six security areas organized under Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.

Each finding connects simulated evidence to a gap, business risk, and proposed improvement with an owner, priority, target, and completion evidence.

## Report
[Read the gap assessment](Garden-State-Sports-Gap-Assessment.pdf)

## Skills demonstrated
- Security gap analysis
- Business risk communication
- Improvement prioritization
- Evidence documentation

## Limitations
All business details and evidence are fictional. No systems were tested, and improvements have not been implemented. Ratings are exercise judgments, not official NIST scores or certification.
